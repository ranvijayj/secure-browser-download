# SecureDL Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026 (v1.2.0)_

SecureDL does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- **Files:** Files you download with SecureDL are fetched by your browser from the website you chose, encrypted on your device, and saved to your computer. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **Passwords:** Passwords you type are used only to encrypt the file, then discarded. They are never stored or transmitted.
- **Settings:** The only things saved are your detection setting, the sites you chose "Always save normally" for, and a short list of your last 15 downloads (file name, site and what SecureDL did), shown under "Recent downloads". All of this is stored locally in your browser and can be cleared in Settings.
- **No analytics, tracking, ads or accounts.** SecureDL makes no network requests of its own beyond fetching the file you asked to download.
- **Website access:** SecureDL asks for no website access at install. When you right-click a link, it uses that tab to fetch the file. It asks for access to a specific site only if needed, and for all sites only if you turn on automatic detection. You can revoke this at any time in `chrome://extensions`.

The source code is public, so you can verify all of the above: see this repository.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
